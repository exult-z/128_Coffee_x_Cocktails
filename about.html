Our Story

Neighborhood cafe | good coffee, good pasta, good vibes | ☕😌

Tucked along Paz Street in Paco, Manila, 1.28 Coffee x Cocktails is your cozy neighborhood spot — a calm café by day and a warm cocktail bar by night. We serve good coffee, good pasta, and good vibes — all wrapped in a plant-filled, wood-and-forest-green space that feels like home.

Upstairs, our reservation-only cocktail bar opens its doors during Cocktail Hours — with live acoustics on Fridays, mellow DJ sets on Saturdays, and Spoken Word Poetry Thursdays for the community.
